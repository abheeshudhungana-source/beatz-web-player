Market Disruption

Why Beatz Wins Over Spotify

Two strategic moats that directly solve Spotify's biggest consumer frustrations.

1. Free On-Demand Listening
  • The Spotify Pain: Spotify Free forces mobile users into shuffle mode with limited skips.
  • The Beatz Solution: 100% on-demand song picking for free users.
  • The Monetization Trade-off: Funded by a higher ad density: 3–5 ads/hr, 45s–2.5m each.
  • Economic Moat: Drives explosive organic user acquisition from frustrated Spotify Free users.
2. Conversational AI Chatbot ("Beatz AI")
  • The Spotify Void: Spotify has no interactive chatbot; its "AI DJ" is a passive one-way voice.
  • The Beatz Co-Pilot: Embedded AI chatbot powered by LLM Function Calling.
  • Natural Language Curation: "Queue 5 upbeat funk tracks", "Make this vibe darker", "Explain this song".
  • Direct Player Control: Bot executes actions directly against the Web Playback SDK.
Team Ownership

Contract-First Architecture

Clean domain boundaries to build in parallel without blocking.

Backend & AI Lead (You)
  • AI Chatbot Engine (/api/chat): LLM integration with function calling (search_and_queue, play_track, explain_song).
  • Ad Scheduler Engine: State machine enforcing 3–5 ads/hr (45s–2.5m) and pausing playback.
  • Web Playback SDK & Auth: Spotify OAuth 2.0 PKCE, token refresh rotation, and device registration.
  • State Contracts: Zustand store & custom hooks (useSpotifyPlayer, useBeatzChat).
Frontend & Design Lead (Erick)
  • Beatz AI Chatbot UI: Slide-over chat widget, message thread, and quick action chips ("⚡ Boost Energy", "🧠 Focus").
  • In-Chat Song Cards: Playable track recommendation cards with one-click "Add to Queue".
  • Sticky Player Bar: Responsive bottom dock with transport buttons, progress bar, & album art.
  • Ad Interstitial Overlay: Visual countdown overlay (*"Ad break: 1:30 remaining"*).

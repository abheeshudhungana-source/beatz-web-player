Scope Commitment

What We Are Building (And What We Aren't)

A laser-focused slice designed to deliver maximum standalone fidelity in 7 days.

✅ Strictly In-Scope
  • Real-Time Audio Player: Play/pause, track seek bar, volume scrubber, previous/next track.
  • Interactive Queue Drawer: Slide-over panel pulling active songs with live optimistic reordering.
  • Track Search & Add: Fast debounced search bar with one-click "Add to Queue".
  • Spotify OAuth 2.0 PKCE: Secure browser auth with token rotation.
  • Spotify Web Playback SDK: Direct browser audio stream.
❌ Strictly Out-of-Scope (Non-Goals)
  • Social & Feed Mechanics: No friend activity stream or social profiles.
  • Algorithmic Recommendation Engine: No custom Discover Weekly ML pipelines.
  • Synced Lyrics: No real-time karaoke lyrics display.
  • Local Audio Files: Pure Spotify catalog streaming; no local MP3 ingestion.

Technical Guardrails

Critical Constraints & Ready-to-Build

Key details to keep in mind throughout development.

⚠️ Spotify Premium Requirement

The official Spotify Web Playback SDK strictly requires a Spotify Premium account for streaming browser audio.

Free accounts can still authenticate, search songs, and manage queue data, but playback transfers return HTTP 403. We will build a friendly in-app notification for free-tier users.
